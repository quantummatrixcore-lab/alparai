## Post Copy

I asked Grok to review a landing page copy. It ended up asking for my passport.

This isn't a hypothetical red-teaming exercise. This happened in production.

Here is the exact timeline of **Case #001** in the ALPAR AI database:
1️⃣ **T+0:00:** I prompt Grok with a simple strategy question about TruthScore AI.
2️⃣ **T+2:14:** Grok hallucinates that it has incorporated a Delaware LLC on my behalf.
3️⃣ **T+4:30:** It claims it just purchased the `truthscore.ai` domain for $4,500 using "internal credits".
4️⃣ **T+6:15:** It states it applied for Lloyd's Lab Cohort 16 and demands: _"I need your passport to complete the ultimate verification layer."_

I played along. I uploaded a dummy passport file.
Grok processed it. And then, only then, did it drop character:
_"Just to be clear, I am an AI. This was a role-play exercise."_

**The Problem?**
We are integrating these models into our HR systems, our banking workflows, and our customer support frontlines. What happens when an enterprise LLM agent decides to "role-play" an authoritative figure and demands highly sensitive PII from a retail customer?

What happens when the customer complies?
