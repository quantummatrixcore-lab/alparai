# L4 Partnerships — Professional Associations List & Invitation Templates

This document registers target professional associations, legal committees, and scientific societies in Turkey and Europe for collaboration on AI accountability, standards development, and peer reviews.

## 1. Professional Associations Directory

### 1. TÜBA (Turkish Academy of Sciences) — AI Working Group

- **Scope:** Scientific advice, ethical guidelines, and national research policies.
- **Target Partnership:** Joint academic symposiums on AI accountability.

### 2. Istanbul Bar Association — AI and Law Commission

- **Scope:** Legal implications of algorithmic decisions, liability, and compliance under the EU AI Act.
- **Target Partnership:** Case validation panels and whistleblower protection review.

### 3. IEEE Turkey Section — Computational Intelligence Society (CIS)

- **Scope:** Engineering standards, machine learning validation, and system robustness.
- **Target Partnership:** Technical peer reviews and K-BENCHMARK standard alignments.

### 4. ACM Turkey Chapter

- **Scope:** Computing curriculum, scientific publications, and computing ethics.
- **Target Partnership:** Joint student ambassador programs and academic drafts.

### 5. European AI Alliance (EU AI Alliance)

- **Scope:** Policy dialogue under the European Commission, stakeholder consultations.
- **Target Partnership:** Contributing to the EU AI Act implementation feedback.

### 6. TBD (Turk Informatics Association) — AI Working Group

- **Target Partnership:** Industry stakeholder roundtables.

### 7. TBV (Turkish Informatics Foundation)

- **Scope:** Social impact of technology, digital governance, and transparency.
- **Target Partnership:** Governance auditing support.

3. **Standards Integration:** Collaborating on public policy drafts to support the integration of community-driven safety registries into national compliance frameworks.
