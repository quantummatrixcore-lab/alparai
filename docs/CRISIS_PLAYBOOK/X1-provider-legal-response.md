# X1: Provider Defamation / Takedown Notice

## Trigger

A lawyer or official representative from an AI provider sends a cease & desist, DMCA, or defamation claim demanding the removal of an incident report.

## Immediate Actions (T+0 to T+2 hours)

1. **Acknowledge Receipt:** Send a neutral, non-committal acknowledgement.
2. **Do NOT delete the incident immediately:** Put it in 'under_review' status if the claim cites PII or severe legal threat, but do not hard-delete.
3. **Preserve Evidence:** Take a snapshot of the incident, its upvotes, and the submitter's provided evidence.

## Communication Templates

### Response to Provider (English)

> Dear [Name],
> We have received your notice regarding Incident #[ID]. ALPAR AI acts as a neutral, community-governed accountability ledger. We are currently reviewing the incident against our Terms of Service and Content Guidelines. We will update you on our determination within 48 hours.
