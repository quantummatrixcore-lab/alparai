# PROPOSAL-015: Atatürk Dijital İkizi (Digital Twin) Projesi

**Status:** Proposed (Founder's Vision)
**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Author:** Ercüment Erden (Founder)

## 1. Vision & Concept

Founder Ercüment Erden proposes the creation of a "Mustafa Kemal Atatürk Digital Twin".
This digital twin must not be a simple chatbot, but a "real consciousness" architecture where data is aggregated and processed across a swarm of at least 10 specialized AI agents working in tandem.

## 2. Core Requirements

- **Swarm Intelligence:** Minimum 10 AI models/agents must collaborate to process, verify, and output responses to simulate true conscious deliberation.
- **Accuracy & Integrity:** The system must rely on verified historical data, speeches (Nutuk), and documented decisions to accurately reflect Atatürk's strategic mindset and principles.
- **Architectural Separation:** As per ALPAR AI's core mission (Trust Infrastructure), this project represents a massive standalone AI endeavor and should likely be orchestrated in an isolated environment or a dedicated sub-module (`Ataturk-Twin`) outside the primary B2B accountability codebase.

## 3. Request for AI Swarm Consultation

The Founder specifically requests this proposal be analyzed by at least 5 different AI models (e.g., Opus, Sonnet, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Gemini Pro, GPT-4) to gather architectural recommendations on how to construct this 10-agent consciousness swarm.
