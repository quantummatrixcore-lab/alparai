
## 4. Logo Clear Space & Minimum Sizing

### 4.1 Clear Space (The "X" Rule)
The clear space around the logo must be at least **0.5 × H** (where H is the height of the shield emblem). No typography, borders, or graphic elements may encroach upon this isolation zone.

### 4.2 Minimum Reproduction Sizes
* **Digital Favicon / Smallest Icon:** 16 × 16 px (Use `alpar-emblem.svg` without subtext).
* **Application Header Emblem:** 24 × 24 px to 32 × 32 px.
* **Full Horizontal Lockup (Digital):** Minimum height 36 px (Width ~195 px).
* **Print Reproduction:** Minimum height 10 mm (Emblem) / 15 mm (Full Lockup).

### 7.2 The Universal Safe Zone Rule
To deploy a single unified design across all platforms:
1. **Left 25% Zone (0px – 600px):** **STRICTLY NO TEXT OR EMBLEM.** Kept exclusively for ambient cybernetic grid background to absorb avatar overlaps on Twitter and LinkedIn.
2. **Golden Center-Right Zone (650px – 2100px):** Houses the Master Emblem, ALPAR AI wordmark, tagline, and compliance badges.
3. **Outer Margins (15% Top/Bottom):** Free of critical information to accommodate YouTube and mobile vertical clipping.
