# ALPAR AI — Product Hunt Launch Plan

> **Goal:** Launch successfully on Product Hunt (PH) to reach 500+ upvotes, gain initial traction, and attract early incident reporters.

---

## 1. Timing & Schedule

- **Target Day:** Tuesday or Wednesday (best days for tech/B2B traction, though competition is higher).
- **Launch Time:** 12:01 AM PST / 10:01 AM TRT (standard Product Hunt day start).
- **Duration:** 24-hour active push cycle.

---

## 2. Key Milestones & Pre-launch (T-14 Days)

- **Milestone 1 (T-14):** Set up the Product Hunt "Coming Soon" teaser page to collect initial subscribers.
- **Milestone 2 (T-10):** Identify and pitch a Hunter (e.g. Chris Messina or Kevin William David) or decide to self-hunt (acceptable in 2026 if list is warm).
- **Milestone 3 (T-7):** Prepare all visual assets (gallery images, logo GIF, 1-minute demo video).
- **Milestone 4 (T-3):** Reach out to personal networks, warm LinkedIn contacts, and the ALPAR Discord/Newsletter subscribers.

---

## 3. Launch Day Checklist (Hour-by-Hour)

- **Hour 0 (12:01 AM PST):** Launch is live. Maker comment posted immediately (story of Case #001).
- **Hour 1-2:** Broadcast to core team, close supporters, and newsletter list. Get initial 50 upvotes to secure top 5 placement.
- **Hour 4 (TRT Morning):** Post native video on LinkedIn explaining the Grok passport story and linking to the launch.
- **Hour 8 (EU Morning):** HackerNews Show HN post (`Show HN: ALPAR AI — trust infrastructure for AI accountability`).
- **Hour 12 (US Morning):** Reddit posts in r/MachineLearning, r/artificial, and r/ArtificialIntelligence.
- **Hour 16 (US Midday):** Follow up with supporters who haven't upvoted yet. Repost on Twitter/X tagging prominent AI safety advocates.
- **Hour 24 (12:00 AM PST):** Thank supporters, publish final numbers in a blog post/newsletter.
