# ALPAR AI Editorial Independence & Neutrality Charter

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** July 11, 2026

## 1. Core Principle

ALPAR AI is committed to absolute neutrality and independence. We exist to build a reliable public registry of AI incidents. Under no circumstances will commercial relationships, payments, sponsorships, or partnership agreements influence the editorial integrity, visibility, severity classification, moderation decisions, or public trust scores on the platform.

## 2. Pinned Responses and Tools Only

All paid tiers on ALPAR AI (e.g., Enterprise Data API, Verified Respondent dashboards) are strictly utility-based. What providers pay for is:

- Coordinated alert systems for new reports.
- Advanced analytics dashboard.
- The capability to post a Pinned Response alongside an incident report.

Payment does **not** buy:

- Redaction, deletion, or moderation of valid incidents.
- Modification of incident severity or Article 73 AI Act risk classifications.
- Artificially boosted Trust Scores.

## 3. Transparency

All Verified Respondent accounts are public and explicitly labeled. All provider responses are displayed as signed, official statements so the public can evaluate their responses alongside user-submitted evidence.
