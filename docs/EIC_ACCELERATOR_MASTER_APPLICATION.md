## PART 1: 12 OFFICIAL EIC DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

### Question 1: What is the breakthrough technological innovation?
**ALPAR AI is the world’s first independent, Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP)-backed AI accountability and regulatory audit infrastructure.** 
Current machine learning evaluation relies on intrusive observability tools (e.g., LangSmith, Arize) that require streaming raw customer data and proprietary weights to third-party US clouds—violating the EU AI Act, GDPR, and sovereign data laws. ALPAR AI decouples verification from data exposure: by implementing zero-knowledge circuits (zk-SNARKs) and deterministic black-box stress-testing, our platform verifies compliance, hallucination bounds, and algorithmic bias mathematically without transferring a single byte of sensitive corporate or training data. It transforms regulatory compliance from a 6-month consulting bottleneck into an automated, verifiable cryptographic certificate.

### Question 2: What is the market pain point and regulatory urgency?
The EU AI Act entered into force in August 2024, imposing strict mandatory compliance deadlines (2025–2026) on high-risk AI applications and General Purpose AI (GPAI) models. Violations incur fines of up to €35M or 7% of global turnover. Concurrently, professional indemnity insurance underwriters now demand third-party algorithmic audit scores before underwriting AI-driven enterprises. However, no automated, neutral third-party rating agency exists for AI; companies are forced to choose between subjective, slow Big-4 consulting audits (€100k+ per audit) or uncertified internal telemetry. ALPAR AI bridges this structural void as the "Moody’s and Deloitte of Artificial Intelligence."

### Question 3: Who is the customer and what is the Unique Value Proposition (UVP)?
Our primary customers are **Tier-1 Enterprise AI deployers, FinTechs, Healthcare providers, and Foundation Model developers** operating in or exporting to the European Single Market.
* **UVP for Enterprises:** Instant, auditable proof of EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 compliance in under 15 minutes, cutting compliance audit expenditure by 85%.
* **UVP for Insurers & Regulators:** Cryptographically signed, immutable audit trails via the proprietary **AI Audit Score™**, enabling automated risk-tier pricing.
* **UVP for Regulated Sovereigns:** Zero data leakage guarantees, enabling public bodies and defense contractors to verify models safely.

### Question 4: How scalable is the business model?
ALPAR AI operates an **Open-Core B2B SaaS and Protocol Licensing model**:
1. **Recurring Enterprise SaaS:** Tiered subscriptions (€2,500 to €15,000/month) for continuous CI/CD automated model auditing and real-time drift detection.
2. **Pay-Per-Audit Certification:** On-demand full accreditation reports for major model release milestones (€5,000 per comprehensive audit).
3. **API Governance Licensing:** High-throughput compliance verification endpoints for insurance carriers and regulatory sandboxes.
The platform scales with zero marginal computational cost per audit due to lightweight local client verifiers and optimized zero-knowledge succinct proof verification.

The Global AI Governance, Compliance, and Risk Management market is projected to surge from **€2.1 Billion in 2024 to €10.8 Billion by 2030 (CAGR 38.4%)**. Capturing just 1.5% of European regulated AI deployments represents a €160M+ annual recurring revenue opportunity.

3. **First-Mover Regulatory Arbitrage:** Direct alignment with EU AI Office testing protocols and NATO standardizations.
