## Thread 1: Launch Thread (English)

1/ Meet ALPAR AI (https://alparai.com) — open-source trust infrastructure for tracking AI incidents and failures in real-time. Why? Because AI accountability shouldn't be a black box. 🧵

2/ We currently track **408 curated and user-reported cases** across **59 distinct AI providers**. From code generation bugs to autonomous driving failures, we are building the historical record of AI behavior.

3/ Under the hood, ALPAR is open-source (AGPL-3.0) and privacy-first. Our PII Guardian scrubs personal identifiers (emails, phones) client-side before any DB write.

4/ We've aligned our data model with the EU AI Act (Art. 73) risk categories. When high-risk incident obligations start on Dec 2, 2027, ALPAR will be ready to act as the primary reporting rail.

5/ Explore the data, read provider responses, and help us build a safer AI ecosystem: https://alparai.com

## Thread 3: Data-Driven Insights (Top Findings)

1/ What does a database of **408 curated and user-reported AI cases** reveal about the current state of AI safety? Here are our top-5 findings from ALPAR AI's incident corpus: 🧵

2/ 1. The average Ecosystem Trust Score for major providers stands at 64/100. Silence is common — many providers simply do not respond to public safety reports.

3/ 2. Hallucinations and incorrect data generation account for 38% of all reported incidents. In critical sectors (finance/medical), these errors carry high real-world costs.

4/ 3. Specific Transparency violations (e.g. models failing to identify themselves as AI to users) are rising, representing 22% of newly logged incidents.

5/ 4. Response rates matter: Verified providers respond to incidents within an average of 4.2 days, whereas unverified providers average >12 days of response latency.

6/ 5. Automated imports and community reporting are scaling the database. Help us document AI incidents to make the future safer: https://alparai.com
