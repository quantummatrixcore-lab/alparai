# PROPOSAL 001: 90-Day Seed Strategy & Monopoly Blueprint

**Author:** Chief Strategy Officer / Systems Architect
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Status:** DRAFT

## Executive Summary

This proposal translates the multi-axis strategic analysis into a 90-day execution plan designed to secure a $1.5M - $3M seed round at a $15M-$20M valuation. The core thesis is positioning ALPAR AI as the "Credit Bureau for AI Accountability" by monopolizing the verification layer of AI incidents.

## The 3-Phase Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation & "Quick Wins" (Days 1 - 30)

**Goal:** Fix the storefront (Vitrin) and convert existing traffic into enterprise leads.

- **Immediate UX Fixes:** Resolve i18n leakage on the homepage, implement actual Trust Score calculations (abandoning the 0% default), and polish micro-interactions (e.g. `active:scale-[0.98]`).
- **EU AI Act Widget:** Embeddable B2B lead magnet widget for AI vendors.
- **Enterprise CTA:** Elevate the `$99/month` and Custom Enterprise pricing directly onto the hero section.

### Phase 2: Open Data Launch & PR Flywheel (Days 30 - 60)

**Goal:** Launch the "First 1000 AI Failures" dataset.

- **Launch Event:** Release the 1000-incident dataset under CC-BY to researchers and media.
- **Streisand Shield PR Loop:** Publicly shame non-responsive providers (e.g. "OpenAI: 46 incidents, 0% response").
- **Journalist Dashboard:** Offer free access to journalists to secure earned media and backlinks.

### Phase 3: Monetization & Seed Pitch (Days 60 - 90)

**Goal:** Secure 3-5 paid B2B pilots and finalize the seed deck.

- **Trust Score API:** Launch the usage-based API ($0.10/call or $5K/mo).
- **Vendor Portal Pro:** Convert claimed provider pages into paying subscribers ($2,500/mo).
- **Pitch Deck:** Finalize the narrative: "S&P/Credit Bureau for AI".

## Moonshot Foundations

- Draft the initial **AI-ISS (AI Incident Severity Score)** RFC to establish the de facto industry standard.
- Begin architectural planning for the **ALPAR Trust Protocol**, a decentralized verification layer utilizing ZK-Proofs.

## Next Steps

1. Immediate execution of Phase 1 UX/UI fixes (i18n, Trust Score algorithm).
2. Begin outreach to journalists for the 1000-incident dataset launch.
