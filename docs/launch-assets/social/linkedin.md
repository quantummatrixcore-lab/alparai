# LinkedIn Launch Announcement (Scheduled: August 2, 2026 at 09:00 AM)

🚀 Today, we are proud to launch **ALPAR AI** (https://alparai.com) — the open-source trust infrastructure for artificial intelligence accountability.

As AI models (such as GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro) are deeply integrated into production systems, their errors, hallucinations, data leaks, and compliance gaps pose significant financial and reputational risks.

ALPAR AI provides a transparent, independent, and crowdsourced registry to document real-time AI incidents, track provider response rates, and calculate empirical Trust Scores.

### 🛡️ What Makes ALPAR AI Unique?

- **AGPL-3.0 Open-Source:** Built transparently with Next.js 15, Supabase, Tailwind v4, and next-intl.
- **PII Guardian:** Built-in client-side privacy regex filters that mask email, phone numbers, and names before they ever reach our database.
- **EU AI Act Alignment:** Incidents are categorized under the EU AI Act risk tiers (Unacceptable Risk, High Risk, etc.) to monitor compliance.
- **Independent Moderation:** Combines automated triage with human expert reviews.

We are launching today with **408 curated incidents** and **59 registered AI providers**.

Join us in building a more accountable AI ecosystem. Report your first incident or explore the code!
