# X5: Regulatory Body Inquiry

## Trigger

An official request for information from a government agency, data protection authority (e.g., KVKK Kurumu), or EU AI Act regulator.

## Immediate Actions

1. **Verify Identity:** Ensure the request is authentically from a government body.
2. **Legal Escort:** All communications must go through the designated legal counsel.
3. **Prepare Export:** Be ready to use exportAuditLogCSV() and exportIncidentsCSV() to provide transparent data.

## Communication Templates

### Acknowledgment

> We acknowledge receipt of your inquiry. ALPAR AI is committed to full compliance with regulatory frameworks including the EU AI Act and KVKK. Our legal team is reviewing your request and will formally respond by [Date].
