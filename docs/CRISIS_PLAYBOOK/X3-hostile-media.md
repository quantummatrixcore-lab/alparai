# X3: Hostile Media Inquiry

## Trigger

A journalist or media outlet sends an inquiry accusing ALPAR AI of bias, manipulation, or being an illegal database.

## Immediate Actions

1. **Do not ignore, do not rush:** Acknowledge receipt immediately, but buy time (e.g., "We will provide a statement by [Time]").
2. **Stick to the Mission:** Emphasize community governance, transparency, and the neutral ledger concept.

## Communication Templates

### Neutral Holding Statement

> Thank you for reaching out. ALPAR AI is a neutral, open-source infrastructure designed to crowdsource and verify AI failures. We do not editorialize or target specific providers. Our moderation logs and datasets are fully transparent. We will review your specific questions and provide a detailed response shortly.
