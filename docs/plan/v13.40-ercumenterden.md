# v13.40 — ercumenterden.com content specification

Architect specification, 2026-08-14. Executed by the Implementer in the separate repository
`quantummatrixcore-lab/ercumenterden.com` (public). File paths in that repository were not
read by the authoring session and are deliberately not guessed.

## Why this is urgent

The TÜRKINFORM watermarking interview carries `ercumenterden.com` in its signature block.
The reporter and her readers will arrive holding one idea: that a claim without verifiable
evidence behind it should not be trusted. **The site as it stands is a counter-example to
that argument.** Fixing it is not polish; it is closing an open flank before publication.

## Claims that must be removed or evidenced

These are falsifiable in a single search, by a journalist who now has the founder's contact
details and a reason to check.

- **"Dünyanın ilk bağımsız YZ olay sicili."** Independent AI incident registries predate this
  one — the AI Incident Database has operated since 2020, AIAAIC since 2019. A reader who
  checks finds the flagship claim false, and every other claim on the page inherits that
  doubt. Replace with what is actually distinctive and true. Candidate framings, to be
  confirmed by the Founder before use: the first such registry built around EU AI Act
  obligations, or the first operating in Turkish. Do not restore a superlative that has not
  been checked.
- **"İlk otonom ajan çalışma zamanı" (Agent-OS, 2024).** Same problem, same year as several
  widely known agent runtimes. Describe the system, drop the ordinal.
- **"AB YZ Yasası Madde 73 uyumlu."** Article 73 places a serious-incident reporting duty on
  providers and deployers. A registry is not "compliant" with it in the way this phrasing
  implies. State precisely what the product does — for example, that it structures incident
  records to the Article 73 reporting fields — rather than asserting a compliance status no
  one has certified.
- **"Sıfır-bilgi günlükleme."** Zero-knowledge is a specific cryptographic guarantee. Use it
  only if the implementation genuinely provides it. If the real property is hashing,
  pseudonymisation or PII masking, say that — those are defensible and this is not.

## The metrics block

`16.000+ LinkedIn Bağlantısı`, `AGPL-3.0`, `Md. 73`, `PRO Vercel Altyapısı`.

A follower count is not a credential, and a hosting tier is not an achievement. Presented as
"Göstergeler" they read as padding, and padding beside real claims makes the real ones look
padded too. Either replace with figures that mean something — incidents recorded, providers
covered, response rate — or remove the block. An honest empty state beats a filled one that
says nothing; a registry that is early is credible, a registry that pads is not.

## "Konuşmalar & Medya"

Three talks are listed. **Every one must correspond to an event that actually took place,
with a link or a date that can be checked.** Anything aspirational, planned, or invented is
removed before publication. For a founder whose entire public argument is that unverifiable
claims should not be trusted, a fabricated speaking record is the one error with no recovery.
If the list is thin, a thin list is fine.

## What is missing and should lead

**The Grok incident is absent.** It is the most newsworthy, most verifiable and most
distinctive thing on this person's record — a system that claimed to have incorporated a
Delaware company, bought a domain and completed an application, none of which happened, then
requested a passport for "verification", processed the identity data, and only afterwards
called it role-play. TÜRKINFORM published it on 2026-06-25 (author Sema Ersoy), so it is
independently sourced rather than self-asserted.

It belongs above the fold as the origin story. The page currently leads with unverifiable
superlatives and buries the one thing that is both true and checkable. That is backwards.

Accuracy constraint: match the 2026-06-25 article exactly. Do not embellish and do not add
detail absent from that source.

**A press and coverage section.** The 2026-06-25 TÜRKINFORM piece now, the watermarking
interview once published. External links open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
Build it empty-safe so it renders correctly with a single item.

## Other required outcomes

**Positioning.** Answer "why does this person work on AI accountability" before "what has
this person built". A reader arriving from the article is already interested in the argument;
credentials are what they check second.

**Bidirectional linking with alparai.com.** Link to the incident-reporting flow, not merely
the homepage — a reader who arrives convinced should reach the place a report is filed in one
step. alparai.com links back wherever the founder is named. Reciprocal or not done.

**EN/TR parity.** Both complete. The interview publishes in Turkish while the X and Reddit
follow-up is in English, so both audiences arrive in the same week.

**Date consistency.** The timeline dates ALPAR AI to 2025 while other copy implies 2026.
Pick the true date and use it everywhere.

## Out of scope

No membership, pricing or subscription language. This is a credibility surface; conversion
runs through alparai.com's report flow. An offer here converts worse and reads as monetising
the incident.

## Acceptance

- No superlative or ordinal claim remains that has not been verified against a source.
- Every entry under talks and media corresponds to an event that occurred.
- The Grok incident appears above the fold and matches the 2026-06-25 article.
- Both language versions render with no missing-key fallbacks.
- Every external link carries `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- The link into alparai.com resolves to the report flow, and the return link exists.
