# FUND-3

**FUND-3** — Non-dilutive funding track, opened before venture conversations.
Owner: Founder, with Architect input on the technical narrative. Priority: P1, after the three gates.

## Why this order

Venture capital is the wrong first door for this project, for reasons that are properties of what has
been built rather than of the market.

**The revenue model is an open question and VC diligence opens with it.** v13.64 established that the
paying party may never be the scored party, which removes the vendor tier and leaves auditors,
insurers and regulators as the customer — none of whom has been sold to. A seed conversation entered
without that answer does not produce a smaller cheque; it produces a no, and the firm remembers.

**The licence and the thesis describe a public registry, not a SaaS.** AGPL-3.0, an open corpus of AI
incidents, and a stated purpose of holding AI systems accountable is exactly the shape that
public-interest funders exist to fund. They do not require a monetisation answer and they take no
equity.

**Investor attention is non-renewable per firm.** The measurement in `MAIL-1` is the evidence: four
cold approaches to serious organisations, sent while the site showed an empty leaderboard and a red
build, produced zero replies in three weeks. Those four introductions are spent.

## The route, in order

**Domestic non-dilutive.** TÜBİTAK's early-stage entrepreneurship support (1512 / BiGG) and its
follow-on investment programme, KOSGEB's R&D and innovation support, and Teknopark incubation with
its associated tax treatment. Twelve state support programmes are already recorded at status `open`
in seed data and need eligibility checked rather than discovered.

**International public-interest and open-source funders.** NGI Zero / NLnet, Mozilla's technology
fund, and the AI-safety and technology-accountability programmes run by research foundations. These
weigh a working public repository and a public-good thesis, both of which exist.

**EU instruments** (Horizon Europe, EIC) where establishment requirements permit — verify eligibility
before investing drafting time, since establishment rules decide this and nothing else does.

**Only then, venture.** `docs/fundraising/vc-outreach-list.md` already maps twenty firms and not one
email has been sent to any of them. That list is an asset precisely because it is unspent.

## Rule for every application

**Verify each programme's current terms, eligibility and deadline at source before drafting.** No
figure, quota or date from this file or from memory enters an application; programme terms change and
a wrong number in an application is the same defect this project documents in others.

## Acceptance

- An eligibility checklist per programme, with the source URL and the date checked
- At least one non-dilutive application submitted, with its date and outcome recorded here
- No venture outreach begins before the three gates are closed and `TRACT-1` produces figures
