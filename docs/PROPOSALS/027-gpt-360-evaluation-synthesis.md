## 2. Core Strategic Recommendation: Single Narrative Umbrella

To eliminate "feature clutter" in investor and customer presentations, ALPAR AI is structured as a single parent platform: **"AlparAI = AI Trust Infrastructure"**.

All features are grouped into 8 modular pillars:

1. **AI Observatory:** Live monitoring of global AI incidents & hallucinations.
2. **AI Evidence:** Immutable evidence vault (SHA-256 ledger) for AI misbehaviors.
3. **AI Benchmark:** K-Benchmark model scoring & comparative analytics.
4. **AI Certification:** EU AI Act & Article 73 compliance verification.
5. **AI Monitoring:** Real-time B2B model safety & prompt injection shields.
6. **AI Risk Intelligence:** Threat modeling & Red Team automated assessments.
7. **AI Transparency Index:** Public tracker for enterprise model disclosures.
8. **AI Trust API:** Programmatic API access to trust scores and verification logs.
