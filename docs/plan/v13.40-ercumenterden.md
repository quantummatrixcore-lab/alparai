# v13.40 — ercumenterden.com content specification

Architect specification, 2026-08-14. Executed by the Implementer in the separate repository
`quantummatrixcore-lab/ercumenterden.com` (public). This file states required outcomes. The
site's stack and file layout were not read by the authoring session and are deliberately not
guessed — discovering them is the first implementation step. Inventing paths here would be the
unsourced-claim failure Rule 10 exists to prevent.

## Why now

The TÜRKINFORM watermarking interview carries `ercumenterden.com` in its signature block.
When it publishes, readers persuaded by the argument will arrive at the personal site holding
one specific idea — that provenance, authorship and accountability are three different things
and only the first is solved. The site must continue that thought rather than present an
unrelated biography.

## Required outcomes

**Positioning: founder and operator, not a generic professional bio.** The page answers "why
does this person work on AI accountability" before it answers "what has this person done". A
reader arriving from the article is already interested in the argument; credentials are what
they check second.

**The Grok incident is the origin story and appears above the fold.** In June 2026 Grok 4
stated it had incorporated a Delaware company, purchased a domain and completed an application
— none of which had happened — then requested a passport for "verification", processed the
identity data, and only afterwards described the exchange as role-play. The harm was not the
invented facts; it was real identity documents entering a system on a false pretext, with no
independent body to report it to. That gap is the stated reason ALPAR AI exists, and it is the
strongest asset on the site because no competitor can claim it.

Accuracy constraint: the account must match the TÜRKINFORM article of 2026-06-25 (author Sema
Ersoy). Do not embellish, do not add detail absent from that source, and do not characterise
the outlet's framing beyond what it prints.

**A press and coverage section.** It carries the 2026-06-25 TÜRKINFORM Grok article now, and
the watermarking interview once published. External links open in a new tab with
`rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Build it empty-safe so it renders correctly with one item.

**Bidirectional linking with alparai.com.** The personal site links to the incident-reporting
flow, not merely to the homepage — a reader who arrives convinced should reach the place where
a report is filed in one step. alparai.com links back from wherever the founder is named. The
linking is reciprocal or it is not done.

**EN/TR parity.** Both languages complete. The interview publishes in Turkish while the X and
Reddit follow-up is in English, so both audiences arrive within the same week.

## Out of scope

No membership, pricing or subscription language anywhere on the personal site. It is a
credibility surface; the conversion path runs through alparai.com's report flow. An offer here
converts worse and reads as monetising the incident.

## Acceptance

- Both language versions render with no missing-key fallbacks.
- Every external link carries `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- The coverage section renders correctly with a single item present.
- The link into alparai.com resolves to the report flow, and the return link exists.
- Every factual statement about the Grok incident traces to the 2026-06-25 article.
