## 2. US AI Safety Institute (US AISI) Engagements

### Email Template

- **Subject:** Integrating Community-Governed AI Safety Data with US AISI Evaluations
- **To:** usaisi@nist.gov (or designated contact)

Dear US AISI Technical Leadership Team,

On behalf of ALPAR AI, I am reaching out to explore a collaborative partnership between our organizations to enhance empirical research into Generative AI model vulnerabilities and real-world failure patterns.

ALPAR AI operates a decentralized, peer-reviewed registry of AI incidents. All reported cases are sanitized through our automated PII Guardian and arbitrated via a multi-provider consensus model to calculate safety ratings (K-BENCHMARK).

Given US AISI's mission under NIST to support the development of trustworthy AI systems and establish measurement standards, we propose:

1. **Open Incident API Access:** Providing US AISI and NIST researchers with unrestricted API endpoints to query historical and real-time AI incident data for policy and risk assessments.
2. **Evaluation Framework Collaboration:** Collaborating on statistical validation methodologies (using our Wilson score interval benchmarks) to measure the real-world deployment safety of commercial foundation models.
3. **Cross-Agency Threat Intel:** Sharing verified red-team exploits and prompt injection vectors discovered by our community bug bounty program.

We look forward to the possibility of sharing our data to strengthen the empirical foundation of AI safety measurements.
