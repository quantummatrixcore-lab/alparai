# Product Hunt Launch Asset

- **Product Name:** ALPAR AI
- **Tagline:** Open-source trust infrastructure for AI accountability
- **Primary Category:** Developer Tools, Open Source, AI/ML
- **Description:**
  ALPAR AI is a public, community-governed incident database tracking real-world AI failures, safety bypasses, and provider responses. Aligned with the EU AI Act (Art. 73) and designed for KVKK/GDPR compliance with an automatic PII Guardian.

---

## Launch Day Screenshots Planner

1. **Home/Dashboard:** Renders the active live feed of incidents, leaderboard of providers, and overall trust score.
2. **Submit Incident:** Show the Turnstile-gated submit flow with markdown editor and Turnstile verification.
3. **EU AI Act Tracker:** Show categories filter (Unacceptable, High Risk, Specific Transparency, Minimal Risk).
4. **Provider Response Page:** Renders a provider's official response linked to their unique response token.
5. **Transparency Report:** The global statistics page showing incident counts, response rates, and user counts.

## First Comment (from Founder)

"Hey Product Hunt! 👋

We are launching ALPAR AI because we believe that AI accountability shouldn't be a black box. As AI systems are deployed in high-risk sectors (medical, legal, financial), tracking their failures is critical for safety and regulation.

We currently host a corpus of **408 curated and user-reported cases** across **59 tracked providers**. Everything we build is open-source (AGPL-3.0) and privacy-first (with automatic client/server PII scrubbing).

We would love to know what other integration points or data filters (like regulatory frameworks, safety metrics) you'd like to see. Thank you for your support!"
