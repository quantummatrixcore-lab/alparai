# X4: Urgent KVKK / GDPR Erasure Demand

## Trigger

A user or third party demands immediate deletion of their Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from an incident report.

## Immediate Actions (T+0 to T+2 hours)

1. **Locate the PII:** Search the database for the reported PII.
2. **Redact or Delete:** If the PII is in the description, edit the incident to mask it. If the incident is entirely based on PII, hard-delete it.
3. **Confirm Erasure:** Reply to the requester confirming the action.

## Communication Templates

### Erasure Confirmation

> We have received your request and have successfully redacted the personal information from our systems in compliance with KVKK/GDPR requirements. The updated incident can be viewed at [URL].
