# ALPAR AI: Trust Infrastructure for AI Accountability

_Empirical Data for AI Risk Analysis, Safety Auditing, and Policy Research_

---

## Executive Summary

ALPAR AI is a public-interest platform and data-repository designed to document real-world AI incidents, model failures, and provider responses. By providing a structured, community-governed ledger of AI behavior, we enable researchers, policymakers, and auditors to analyze empirical risk distribution across models and systems.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. The Incident Corpus

- **Incident Dataset:** Over **408 curated and user-reported cases** across **59 tracked AI providers**.
- **Historical Depth:** Continuous tracking of algorithmic errors, safety bypasses, privacy violations, and physical accidents.
- **Categorization:** Mapped directly to the EU AI Act risk categorizations (Unacceptable, High Risk, Specific Transparency, Minimal) and serious incident classifications.

### 2. Privacy-First Architecture (PII Guardian)

- **Automatic Masking:** Fully-realized client-side and server-side guardian patterns automatically scrub personally identifiable information (PII) like names, emails, phones, and credit cards before database write.
- **KVKK / GDPR Alignment:** Designed to adhere to global privacy frameworks.

### 3. Open API & F2 Portal

- **Developer Access:** Rest-based developer APIs providing structured JSON outputs of incidents, providers, and statistical indicators.
- **Open Science:** Promoting reproducible research by making the entire code repository open-source (AGPL-3.0).
