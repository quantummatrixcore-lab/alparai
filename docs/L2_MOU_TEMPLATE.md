## Academic & Research Collaboration on AI Trust and Safety Infrastructure

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is entered into as of the date of last signature below ("Effective Date"), by and between:

1. **ALPAR AI Trust Infrastructure** (represented by its governance body / founding entity, hereinafter referred to as "ALPAR AI"),
2. **[PARTNER UNIVERSITY NAME]**, acting on behalf of its **[Department / Research Center Name]** (hereinafter referred to as the "Academic Partner").

ALPAR AI and the Academic Partner shall collectively be referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

---

### 1. Purpose & Objectives

The purpose of this MOU is to establish a non-binding framework for academic and research collaboration between the Parties to advance scientific methodologies in AI safety assessment, AI incident tracking, and algorithmic audit validation.

Specific cooperation objectives include:

- **AI Safety & Incident Taxonomy:** Collaboration on mapping AI incidents against the EU AI Act Art. 73 and KVKK standards.
- **K-BENCHMARK Rubrics:** Contributing to the development and peer review of evaluation rubrics across safety, factuality, and ethical reasoning categories.
- **Methodology Advisory:** Participating in academic peer reviews of debate transcripts generated by the ALPAR cross-model debate engine.

---

### 2. Areas of Collaboration

The Parties intend to cooperate in the following areas:

#### 2.1. Faculty Fellowship & Guest Lecturing

The Academic Partner's researchers and faculty members may apply to the ALPAR Faculty Fellowship program to conduct joint research on AI transparency datasets.

#### 2.2. Student Ambassador & Research Internships

Graduate and undergraduate students from the Academic Partner may participate as ALPAR Student Ambassadors, assisting in community validation and documentation projects under academic supervision.

#### 2.3. Independent Audit & Validation Committees

Qualified academic staff from the Partner may be invited to join the ALPAR Methodology Advisory Committee (L9) to perform annual independent audits of the evaluation methodologies.

### 4. Confidentiality & Data Protection (GDPR / KVKK)

The Parties agree to handle all shared data in compliance with GDPR and KVKK (Turkish Data Protection Law) regulations.

- No personally identifiable information (PII) of incident reporters, users, or research subjects shall be shared under this agreement.
- Any shared research datasets must be anonymized and masked using the ALPAR PII Guardian gateway prior to transmission.

---

### 5. Non-Binding Nature

This MOU is non-binding and does not constitute a legally binding contract or financial obligation on either Party. Any specific project, resource allocation, or joint funding initiative shall be subject to separate, legally binding agreements.

---

### 6. Term and Termination

This MOU shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. Either Party may terminate this MOU at any time by providing thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party.
