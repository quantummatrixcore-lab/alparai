## 2. Description Copy

### Short Description (260 characters max)

> ALPAR AI is the first community-governed platform for documenting, verifying, and permanently recording AI failures, hallucinations, and privacy violations. When an AI lies, ALPAR keeps the record — ensuring transparency and model accountability.

### Long Description (Markdown formatted)

```markdown
AI is entering hospitals, courtrooms, schools, and banks at breakneck speed. Yet, when an AI fabricates a company, requests sensitive passport documents, or halluncinates a medical diagnosis, there is no independent, public record. AI providers write their own safety reports.

ALPAR AI exists because humanity needs an independent scoreboard.

We are an open-source (AGPL-3.0), community-governed registry for AI incidents. No login required, no tracking, PII automatically masked on submit.

### Why ALPAR AI?

- **Community Governance:** Governed by people, not AI boardrooms.
- **Trust Scores:** AI providers are ranked by their response rates and resolution times.
- **PII Guardian:** Auto-masks sensitive personal information before it ever hits our database.
- **Verified Respondents:** Allows AI companies to officially pin statements and counter-evidence on reported cases.

Every documented incident protects the next person. Audit the code, report an incident, and help build the global standard for AI accountability.
```

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: How do you prevent fake reports?**
  - _A:_ Every incident undergoes a multi-layer verification process. Community moderators review evidence, and our backend automatically cross-audits the report against five independent LLM models to flag potential sycophancy or fake submissions.
- **Q: Are you against AI development?**
  - _A:_ Not at all. We believe AI has immense potential, but accountability is a prerequisite for safety. High-quality telemetry of failures is how industries (like aviation) become safe.
- **Q: How do you protect user privacy?**
  - _A:_ Our built-in PII Guardian automatically detects and redacts emails, credit cards, passport numbers, and phone numbers in the browser/API boundary before data is committed to PostgreSQL.
