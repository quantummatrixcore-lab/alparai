# v13.50 — Investment readiness: Yapay Zekâ Fabrikası

Architect assessment, 2026-08-17, on the Founder's question: should ALPAR AI apply to Yapay Zekâ
Fabrikası, and is the system ready?

**Verdict: the thesis is fundable and the timing is real. The codebase would not survive technical
diligence today. The gap is one to two weeks of cleanup, not a rebuild.**

## The programme

Türkiye İş Bankası's corporate venture capital arm and acceleration programme, investing in AI
startups with momentum and global growth potential, requiring at least one Turkish founder. Support
includes guidance on TÜBİTAK, Ministry of Commerce and KOSGEB grants, plus cloud and AI
infrastructure through AWS, Microsoft, Google and IBM.

Eligibility is met on every stated criterion. Readiness is the open question.

## Why readiness is not a matter of judgement here

The repository is **public under AGPL-3.0**. Technical diligence does not need to ask for access,
and does not need to trust a summary. Whatever state the tree is in on the day of application is the
state the reviewer reads. That removes the usual gap between what a pitch claims and what can be
checked — in both directions.

## What blocks it, in the order a reviewer encounters it

**1. The build is red.** `pnpm typecheck` reports 36 errors across 13 files. Five files carry
`@ts-nocheck`, which disables type checking for the entire file — three of them public API routes
(`slopsquatting`, `trust-ranking`, `webhooks`). The 36 is the count _with_ those five excluded.
`eslint-disable` stands at 23, up from 15 before the recent repair. → `TYPE-2`

**2. Eighty-three tracked files at the repository root**, of which eight are legitimate
configuration. The rest is 1.4 MB of build output, scraped HTML and superseded copies of a generated
file: `typecheck_output_old.txt` (547 KB), `database_new.ts` (339 KB), `founder_panel_v2_raw.html`
(173 KB), `database_old.ts` (109 KB), screenshots, CDP scraping scripts. Scanned for credential
patterns — zero matches, so this is hygiene rather than disclosure. It is nonetheless the first
directory anyone opens. → `REPO-1`

**3. CI has never produced a successful run.** The GitHub Actions quota is exhausted and 21
workflows trigger on every push with no path filters. The Actions tab shows an unbroken wall of red.
→ `CI-COST-1`, plus the quota itself, which is Founder-side

**4. The corpus size is unmeasured.** "992 incidents" appears in strategy material while a
page-level count reported 371. A data-asset pitch resting on a figure the team cannot reproduce on
request is where diligence stops. → `STRAT-1`

**5. `case_001.status` claims "Under Investigation by KVKK, EU Commission."** Six of the seven
bodies have acknowledged nothing. A reviewer may contact them directly. The claim should be sourced
or changed to "filed, awaiting response" — which is true, verifiable, and still strong.

## A correction to this project's own record

The board carried a paragraph stating the authentication bypass was **not** contained, and that the
three call sites read `process.env.IS_PLAYWRIGHT_TEST` directly. **That is stale and wrong**, as the
table above records. It was copied forward into the v13.47 restructure without re-measurement — the
same defect this board keeps correcting in others, committed by the Architect this time. Anyone
reading the board as a security summary would have been misled about the project's most sensitive
control.

## Sequence

1. **`TYPE-2`** — green build, `@ts-nocheck` back to zero. Nothing else matters while the build is red.
2. **`REPO-1`** — the root cleanup. Mechanical, and the largest improvement in first impression per
   hour spent.
3. **Correct the two claims** — the auth-bypass board entry (done here) and `case_001.status`.
4. **`STRAT-1`** — one query, one number, one date. Every figure in the application traces to it.
5. **`CI-COST-1`** and the Actions quota — one green run visible on the Actions tab.

Items 1–3 are the hard gate. Items 4–5 are cheap and make the application materially stronger.

## Recommendation

Apply after the gate, not before. Nothing in the blocker list is architectural; all of it is
unfinished cleanup with a named owner and an executable acceptance test. The thesis, the regulatory
record and the Article 73 timing are the assets, and they are real today.
