## 4. Competitive Finding

ALPAR AI's specific niche—a public, incident-level accountability registry—has no funded commercial rival.

The closest comparables are the AI Incident Database (AIID, 2000+ reports across 400+ incidents) and AIAAIC, both of which are non-commercial. AIID is philanthropy/institution-funded and explicitly states that its sustainability path involves **"partnering with the broader Governance, Risk, and Compliance industry."** This is precisely the B2B Risk API direction already scoped and documented in ALPAR AI's Architecture Decision Records (ADR).

Rather than facing direct commercial competition, ALPAR AI is pursuing a monetization path that non-commercial peers acknowledge as the viable route to sustainability.
